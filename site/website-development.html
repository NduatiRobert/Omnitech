FAQ

Websites FAQs

Straight answers to the questions we hear most.

Ask us a question
How long does it take to build a website?

A standard business website usually takes 2 to 4 weeks once we have your content. E-commerce and larger custom websites take longer; we agree a timeline up front.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, features (such as online payments or bookings) and content needed. We give you a fixed quotation after understanding your requirements.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We can build on an easy-to-use content management system and train you to update text, images, products and blog posts.

Do you provide hosting and a domain name?

Yes. We can register your domain, set up reliable hosting and create professional email addresses such as you@yourbusiness.co.ke.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every website we build is fully responsive and tested on phones, tablets and desktops.

Will my website appear on Google?

We set up the technical SEO foundations and submit your site to Google. Ranking higher for competitive searches takes ongoing SEO work, which we also offer.

Can you accept M-Pesa payments on my website?

Yes. We integrate M-Pesa and card payments for online stores, bookings and invoices.
