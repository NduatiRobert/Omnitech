FAQ

Internet & Networking FAQs

Straight answers to the questions we hear most.

Ask us a question
Are you an internet service provider (ISP)?

We are network installers and IT consultants. We help you choose a suitable internet provider, then install, configure and optimise the connection and the network inside your premises.

Why is my Wi-Fi slow in some rooms?

Usually it is router placement, walls, interference or a single router trying to cover too much space. A site survey shows exactly where coverage drops and what will fix it.

Can you connect two buildings without digging trenches?

Yes. Point-to-point wireless links can connect buildings that have line of sight, often kilometres apart, without laying cables.

Do you do structured cabling for new offices?

Yes. We plan and install Cat6 cabling, data points, network cabinets and patch panels, and label everything for easy maintenance.

Can you limit what staff or guests can access?

Yes. We configure separate networks, content filtering and bandwidth limits so guests and staff only get what they need.

Do you offer support contracts?

Yes. We offer monthly support plans with monitoring, troubleshooting and on-site visits, as well as one-off call-outs.
