FAQ

CCTV & Security FAQs

Straight answers to the questions we hear most.

Ask us a question
How much does a CCTV installation cost?

Cost depends on the number of cameras, camera type, cable distances and how many days of footage you want to store. We do a free site survey and give you a clear, itemised quotation before any work starts.

Can I watch my cameras on my phone?

Yes. Every system we install is set up for remote viewing on Android and iPhone, and on a laptop. We install the app for you and show you how to view live and recorded footage.

How long is footage stored?

Typically 2 to 4 weeks, but it depends on the hard-drive size, number of cameras and recording quality. We size storage to match how far back you need to look.

Do the cameras work at night?

Yes. We install cameras with infrared or colour night vision so you get usable footage in low light and complete darkness.

What happens when there is a power blackout?

We recommend a UPS or power backup so cameras and the recorder keep running during outages. We size the backup to your system.

Which camera brands do you install?

We work with reputable, widely supported brands such as Hikvision and Dahua, and recommend the right model for each location and budget.

Do you offer maintenance after installation?

Yes. We offer maintenance contracts covering inspections, cleaning, firmware updates, repairs and replacements, plus support when you need footage retrieved.
