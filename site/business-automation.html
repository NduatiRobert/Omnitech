FAQ

Business Automation FAQs

Straight answers to the questions we hear most.

Ask us a question
What kind of tasks can be automated?

Common examples include sending invoices and payment reminders, approving requests, updating records across apps, generating reports and responding to frequently asked questions.

Do I need to change the software I already use?

Usually not. We connect and automate the tools you already have wherever possible, and only recommend new tools when they add clear value.

How do we start?

We begin with a short process review to identify the tasks that take the most time, then automate the highest-value ones first.

Is automation expensive?

Many automations pay for themselves quickly by saving staff hours and reducing errors. We scope projects in small stages so you see value early.

Can you automate WhatsApp messages?

Yes. We can set up automated WhatsApp notifications and chatbots using approved WhatsApp Business solutions.

What if an automation breaks?

We build in monitoring and alerts, and offer support plans to fix issues and adjust automations as your processes change.
