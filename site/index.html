What services does Omnitech Systems offer?

We install CCTV and security systems, internet and Wi-Fi networks and hotspot billing systems, and we build websites, custom software, digital marketing campaigns, business automations and Zoho solutions.

Do you work with both homes and businesses?

Yes. We serve homeowners, small businesses, schools, estates and larger organisations.

Do you offer free site surveys?

Yes. For CCTV, networking and hotspot projects we offer a site survey so that your quotation is accurate.

Where are you located and which areas do you serve?

Our office is at Montana Mall in Nairobi CBD, and we serve clients in all cities and towns across Kenya. Depending on the project, we also work remotely with clients in Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, the UAE and other countries, especially for websites, software, digital marketing, automation and Zoho projects.

How do I get a quote?

Send us a message on WhatsApp, call us or fill in the contact form. Tell us what you need and we will get back to you quickly.

Do you provide support after the project?

Yes. We offer maintenance and support plans for every service, as well as one-off support visits.
